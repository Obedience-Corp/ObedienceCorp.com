Thesis

Why we exist.

Who We Are

Obedience Corp develops cognitive infrastructure that compresses the coordination burden of complex work.

We build systems that let highly capable individuals and small teams do work that previously required large organizations, years of coordination, and significant capital.

Our flagship product, Festival, is a hierarchical planning system that gives autonomous AI agents structure across multiple projects. One command — fest next — always returns the next actionable step. The system is released, source-available, and CLI-native.

Why Now

AI agents can write code, answer questions, and execute commands. What they can’t do is coordinate across projects, maintain context over time, or know what to do next without being told.

This is the coordination bottleneck. As agents get more capable, the problem gets worse — not better. More agents across more projects means more context to manage, more state to track, and more decisions about what should happen next. The tools that exist today solve pieces of this for single projects. None solve it across a portfolio.

The market is moving fast. GSD reached 46,000 GitHub stars in three months proving developers want structured AI workflows. 8090 Software Factory signed Ernst & Young proving enterprises will pay for AI workflow orchestration. Cursor passed $2 billion in annualized revenue proving the AI development tools market is massive.

The gap between these products is the gap we fill. Deeper than GSD. Open and affordable versus 8090. Infrastructure-grade, not a wrapper.

The window for establishing the hierarchical orchestration layer is open now. It will not stay open. We intend to fill it.

Evidence

Results from one person using the system:

  • 27 projects managed in one campaign
  • 327x measured productivity leverage
  • 1.1M lines of code under management
  • 63 completed festivals
  • $170K revenue generated using the system
