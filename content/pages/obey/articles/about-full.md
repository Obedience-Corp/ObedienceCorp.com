Obey is the execution layer for AI agents. While Festival structures what work needs to happen, Obey handles how that work gets done — managing agent sessions, enforcing security, metering usage, and syncing state in real-time.

It runs as a local daemon on your machine, exposing a control plane that any client can connect to: CLI, TUI, mobile, or web. Agents from any provider — Claude Code, Codex, OpenAI, or custom tooling — run as managed sessions with full lifecycle control.

The result is executive-level oversight of your AI operations. You set direction. Obey handles execution. Every action audited. Every boundary enforced.

**Your agents. Your rules. Your runtime.**
