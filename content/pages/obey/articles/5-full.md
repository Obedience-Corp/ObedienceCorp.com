Obey runs as a background daemon on your development machine. Clients connect over a local gRPC socket or remotely via the obey.app hub.

This means you can start an agent session from your laptop, walk away, and check on it from your phone. Activity streams in real-time. Session controls work from any client.

The hub synchronization layer maintains a WebSocket connection to obey.app, projecting your campaign state, festival progress, and agent activity to the cloud dashboard. Auto-reconnect with configurable backoff ensures reliability.

TUI for power users. Web for visibility. Mobile for on-the-go monitoring. Same daemon. Same state. Any surface.

**Your runtime, accessible from everywhere.**
